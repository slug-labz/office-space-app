Office Space — how to install it, and the one bit of friction
==============================================================

1. Drag Office Space onto the Applications folder in this window.


2. Open it. The first time, macOS will very likely refuse. You will get one
   of these two messages, depending on which Mac you have:

       "Office Space" is damaged and can't be opened.
       You should move it to the Trash.
                                            — Apple Silicon (M1 and later)

       "Office Space" can't be opened because it is from an
       unidentified developer.
                                            — Intel

   The app is not damaged and nothing went wrong with your download.

   macOS flags everything that arrives through a browser, and it lifts that
   flag by itself only for apps signed by Apple. Signing means an Apple
   Developer membership at $99 a year. This project has not bought one, so
   the app arrives unsigned and macOS says the most alarming thing it knows
   how to say about it.

   That is the whole story. It is a missing receipt, not a virus warning.


3. Clear the flag once. Either of these works, and you only ever do it once.

   THE EASY WAY

       Right-click "Fix permissions.command" in this window, and choose Open.

       Right-click, not double-click. Double-clicking gets you the same
       refusal — the script is unsigned too. Right-click and Open gets you a
       dialog with an Open button on it, and that button is the point.

       It prints what it found and what it changed, so you can see it did
       exactly one thing.

   THE TYPING WAY

       Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line,
       and press return:

           xattr -cr "/Applications/Office Space.app"

   Both do the same thing: remove the download flag from the copy you just
   put in Applications. Nothing is installed, nothing goes over the network,
   and nothing outside that one app is touched.

   Then open Office Space normally. It will keep opening normally.


One thing worth knowing
-----------------------

The usual advice for unsigned apps — right-click the app and choose Open —
gets you past the "unidentified developer" message but NOT past the "damaged"
one. If right-clicking the app did nothing for you, that is why, and step 3
is the fix.


Why we did not just buy the certificate
---------------------------------------

Because it would be the only thing in this project that costs money, and it
buys a smoother first thirty seconds rather than a better app. It is one
purchase away whenever it is worth it, and until then this page is us being
straight with you about it instead of hoping you do not notice.

Office Space spawns the `claude` you already have installed. There is no
server, no account and no API key.
